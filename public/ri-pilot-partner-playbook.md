# Rhode Island Pilot Partner Playbook

**SmartProBonoIP · Preparation only — not legal advice**

One-page guide for innovation hubs, clinics, and libraries introducing inventors to SmartProBonoIP.

## What SmartProBonoIP is

SmartProBonoIP helps inventors organize invention information into an **IP Readiness Packet** before meeting a patent professional, IP clinic, PTRC, mentor, or innovation partner. It does not provide legal advice, patentability opinions, or filing services.

## Who it helps

- Inventors with rough sketches or notes but no organized summary
- Founders preparing for office hours, pitch events, or clinic intake
- Creators and small businesses unsure how to explain their idea to an expert

## What inventors get (~14 minutes)

- Five-step wizard with save & continue later — no account required
- Plain-language idea summary and readiness snapshot
- Development timeline, materials checklist, and expert questions
- Similar reference search prep with Google Patents-first guidance
- PDF download and optional Export for Attorney (JSON/CSV)

## 30-second introduction script

> Before your clinic or mentor meeting, try SmartProBonoIP. It helps you organize your idea in plain language — what you built, who it's for, timeline notes, and questions to bring. It's free, takes about fourteen minutes, and it's preparation only — not legal advice. Start at smartprobonoip.org or ask us for a referral link.

## Recommended partner flow

1. Share your tracked referral link or QR code.
2. Point inventors to **View sample packet** if they want to see the HydroSeal demo.
3. Encourage **Start with this example** for a pre-filled walkthrough.
4. Ask them to download the PDF and bring it to their next conversation.
5. Optional: have them save 1–3 similar references in Search Prep before the meeting.

## What partners should NOT say

- Do not say SmartProBonoIP tells users whether an idea is patentable.
- Do not promise free legal representation or pro bono attorney matching.
- Do not treat CPC suggestions or search queries as classification opinions.
- Do not share a user's private recovery link.

## Pilot metrics

- Packet completions and clarity lift
- PDF downloads and recovery link usage
- Common readiness signals and support needs
- Partner-attributed referrals
