## Allowed values

**Type:** `inventor` · `founder` · `attorney` · `IP analyst` · `org`

**Source:** `Reddit` · `warm_intro` · `referral` · `email` · `local`

**Status:** `contacted` · `replied` · `testing` · `completed` · `feedback received`

**Packet completed / PDF downloaded / Permission to quote:** `yes` · `no`

## How to use

1. Log each person **before** sending more testers — this turns outreach into proof.
2. Update status as they reply, start testing, finish a packet, or send feedback.
3. Mark packet/PDF completion from what they report (or from partner desk metrics when available).
4. Only set **Permission to quote** to `yes` when they explicitly agree.
5. Export or duplicate this sheet before handing off to another teammate.
