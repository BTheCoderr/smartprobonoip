# SmartProBonoIP Hyperagent Briefing

## Purpose

Use this file as the source-of-truth briefing for a rigorous zero-to-$100,000 growth plan.

SmartProBonoIP is not seeking a generic startup plan. The goal is to identify the fastest credible route to paying organizational customers, measurable product proof, and repeatable inventor referrals.

## Company and product

SmartProBono is a legal technology company based in Providence, Rhode Island.

SmartProBonoIP helps inventors, founders, and small businesses turn unstructured invention stories into organized IP Readiness Packets before professional review.

The product currently helps organize:

1. The problem being solved
2. The proposed solution
3. Key technical features
4. Possible alternatives and variations
5. Inventor and ownership information
6. Supporting materials
7. Known information gaps
8. Questions for the next professional conversation
9. A professional-facing reviewer summary

SmartProBonoIP does not provide legal advice, legal representation, patentability opinions, clearance opinions, filing services, or replace a licensed attorney or registered patent practitioner.

## Current operating rules

2. Do not recommend major product development unless at least three independent users or partners identify the same recurring problem.
3. Do not lead marketing with AI.
4. Lead with reduced intake friction, reduced unpaid preparation, better prepared inventors, and better professional handoffs.
5. Distinguish facts, assumptions, hypotheses, and recommendations.
6. Cite reliable sources for market, competitor, pricing, and regulatory claims.
7. Never invent customer quotes, partnerships, traction, or revenue.
8. Prefer actions with owners, deadlines, target numbers, and pass-or-fail criteria.
9. Challenge weak assumptions rather than agreeing automatically.
10. Do not treat unpaid pilots, grants not yet awarded, unsigned partnerships, or hypothetical valuation as revenue.
