# SmartProBonoIP Stage 1 Scorecard

## Stage objective

Prove that SmartProBonoIP creates better prepared inventors and more useful professional intake handoffs before expanding the feature set.

## Stage 1 targets

| Metric | Target | Current | Definition |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Completed real inventor packets | 100 | 0 | A non-demo user completes the core intake and produces a packet. |
| IP professional pilot partners | 10 | 0 | A patent professional, firm, clinic, university program, or inventor-support organization agrees to refer users or review packets. |
| Paying organizations | 5 | 0 | An organization pays for a pilot, sponsored access, or recurring program access. |
| Strong case studies | 3 | 0 | A documented before-and-after story with permission, measurable value, and a professional or organizational quote. |
| Partner referral rate | 30% | 0% | Percentage of activated pilot partners that refer at least one additional inventor after their first referral. |

## North star metric

Completed packets that a professional reviewer says saved meaningful intake or preparation time.

## Activation definitions

### Inventor activated

The inventor completes the problem, proposed solution, and key-feature sections.

### Packet completed

The inventor produces a packet containing the core invention summary, known gaps, supporting information, and questions for the next conversation.

### Pilot partner activated

The partner refers at least one real inventor or reviews at least one real packet.

### Paying organization activated

Payment is received and at least one program administrator or professional begins using the partner workflow.

## Weekly operating metrics

Track every Monday:

1. New partner conversations
2. Pilot partners committed
3. Partners activated
4. Inventors referred
5. Intakes started
6. Core intakes completed
7. Packets generated
8. Packets reviewed by professionals
9. Packets rated useful
10. Estimated professional time saved
11. Repeat partner referrals
12. Pilot or subscription revenue

## Four-week launch targets

### Week 1 — Launch foundation

- Publish the focused pilot landing page
- Update LinkedIn logo, banner, tagline, overview, industry, and specialties
- Record the 60-second demo
- Prepare the first 50-person outreach list
- Confirm the first two warm pilot conversations

### Week 2 — Partner conversations

- Contact 50 targeted professionals and organizations
- Secure at least 10 conversations
- Confirm five founding pilot partners
- Give every partner a unique tracked referral link

### Week 3 — Real packet activity

- Refer the first 20 inventors
- Generate at least 10 real packets
- Collect structured reviewer feedback
- Record time-saved estimates and missing-information feedback

### Week 4 — Conversion and proof

- Convert the strongest pilots into paid engagements
- Publish the first approved case study
- Identify the three most repeated professional workflow requests
- Decide what to build next based on repeated evidence, not isolated requests

## Case study evidence template

- Partner type
- Inventor type
- Intake condition before SmartProBonoIP
- Packet sections completed
- Missing information identified
- Reviewer time before and after
- What the reviewer found useful
- What remained missing
- Whether the partner would refer another inventor
- Approved quote
- Permission status

## Build rule

Do not add a major feature unless at least three pilot partners independently identify the same recurring problem or the data shows a clear conversion or completion bottleneck.
