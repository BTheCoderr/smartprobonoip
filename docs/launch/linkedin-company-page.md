## Overview

SmartProBono builds preparation and intake infrastructure for inventors, founders, small businesses, and the professionals who support them.

Our current product, SmartProBonoIP, helps users turn an early-stage invention story into an organized IP Readiness Packet before a conversation with a patent professional, legal clinic, mentor, university program, innovation hub, or other qualified resource.

The guided workflow helps users describe the problem, proposed solution, key features, possible alternatives, inventor and ownership information, supporting materials, and unanswered questions in one structured handoff. The goal is a better prepared inventor and a more useful first professional conversation.

SmartProBonoIP is currently entering a Rhode Island founding partner pilot with patent professionals, clinics, universities, incubators, and inventor-support organizations.

SmartProBonoIP is an educational readiness and organization tool. It does not provide legal advice, legal representation, patentability opinions, clearance opinions, or replace a licensed attorney or registered patent practitioner.
