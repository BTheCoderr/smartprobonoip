# SmartProBonoIP 60-Second Product Demo

## Recording goal

Show one transformation: an inventor begins with a messy idea and finishes with an organized packet that is easier for a professional to review.

Do not lead with AI. Do not claim legal conclusions, patentability, or filing readiness.

## Narration and shot list

### 0:00–0:06 — The problem

**Screen:** SmartProBonoIP homepage, then move toward Start.

**Voiceover:**

“Most inventors do not begin with a polished intake. They begin with scattered notes, explanations, and unanswered questions.”

### 0:06–0:15 — Inventor begins intake

**Screen:** Open the guided intake and show the first core questions.

**Voiceover:**

“SmartProBonoIP guides the inventor through the essential story: the problem, the proposed solution, and what makes the idea different.”

### 0:15–0:27 — Inventor explains the idea

**Screen:** Enter a short sample response and move through features, alternatives, inventor information, and supporting materials.

**Voiceover:**

“The inventor explains the idea in their own words while the workflow organizes key features, possible variations, ownership details, and supporting materials.”

### 0:27–0:37 — Missing information is identified

**Screen:** Show the packet preview, readiness gaps, follow-up prompts, or expert questions.

**Voiceover:**

“Before the handoff, SmartProBonoIP surfaces missing information and prepares clearer questions for the next professional conversation.”

### 0:37–0:49 — Packet is produced

**Screen:** Generate and scroll through the IP Readiness Packet. Briefly show the problem, solution, features, gaps, and reviewer summary.

**Voiceover:**

“The result is an organized IP Readiness Packet that brings the inventor’s information into one structured, review-ready handoff.”

### 0:49–0:57 — Professional receives the summary

**Screen:** Show the professional-facing packet summary or dashboard view.

**Voiceover:**

“The professional receives a better prepared inventor without having to rebuild the entire intake from zero.”

### 0:57–1:00 — Call to action

**Screen:** Pilot landing page and founding partner CTA.

**Voiceover:**

“We are selecting five founding pilot partners. Join us at SmartProBono.org slash pilot.”

## On-screen closing text

Better prepared inventors.

More useful IP intake.

Become a SmartProBonoIP pilot partner.

## Recording notes

Use the existing demo mode and sample invention so no confidential information appears.

Keep the cursor movement deliberate and remove loading delays during editing.

Record the screen in landscape format at 1080p.

Use captions throughout because many LinkedIn viewers watch without sound.

Keep the disclaimer visible at the end:

“Educational readiness tool. Preparation only — not legal advice.”
