# SmartProBono: first seven pilot conversations

**Owner:** Founder. **Status:** Outreach-ready materials, not proof of participants, referrals, or permission to contact anyone.

## Goal

Run five voluntary inventor usability sessions and two professional reviews of a fictional invention-preparation packet. Test whether Learn → Prepare → Connect makes the next conversation more organized. Record outcomes without publishing personal or invention details.

## What to link

- Public product overview: https://smartprobono.org/
- Fictional packet: https://smartprobono.org/sample
- Pilot overview: https://smartprobono.org/pilot
- Internal pilot outreach tracker: https://smartprobono.org/pilot-tracker

Check the links on the live site before sending. The tracker is **local to one browser**; export CSV for your private files as needed. Do not commit names, emails, notes, inventions, or reviewer responses to the repository.

## Recruiting sequence (not automated sending)

1. Founder chooses recipients and approves exact wording and channel.
2. Ask for consent to participate in a **15-minute feedback session**. Do not request a confidential invention idea or unpublished disclosure through an email, public form, GitHub Issue, or shared spreadsheet.
3. Ask the person to use the publicly fictional HydroSeal sample initially. They can evaluate usability without sharing their own invention.
4. Record agreement and availability **outside GitHub** using an approved private system with an appropriate privacy notice; the public GitHub issue should include aggregate counts only.
5. Invite a second professional to independently assess the same sample.
6. Run the sessions using [PILOT_SESSION_GUIDE.md](./PILOT_SESSION_GUIDE.md); summarize with [PILOT_MEASUREMENT.md](./PILOT_MEASUREMENT.md).
7. Only after security/privacy review may founder invite real submissions of invention facts. A successful browser smoke test is not sufficient approval for processing confidential ideas.

## Copy-ready inventor invitation

Hi! I'm testing SmartProBono, a tool that helps inventors learn the basics of IP readiness, organize what they have, and prepare questions before meeting an IP professional.

I'm looking for a few volunteers to spend about 15 minutes reviewing a fictional example and walking through the experience. I'm interested in what makes sense, what feels confusing, and what you'd want improved. You don't need to share your own invention or private information to help.

If you're interested, reply and I'll share the sample and a few questions. Participation is completely optional.

## Pilot rules

- No mass DM automation, cold email scraping, paid ad spend, or claiming an endorsement.
- Never claim an inventor is a customer or a professional is a pilot partner until the involvement is verified and any public use is authorized.
- Do not send a real inventor packet to a reviewer without clear permission, secure delivery, and any appropriate professional confidentiality safeguards.
- IP education and preparation only; not legal advice, patentability determination, filing, representation, or guaranteed legal services.
- Respect declined invitations, feedback withdrawal requests, and existing privacy/deletion processes.
