# Pilot session guide: 15 minutes

**What we are testing:** clarity, completion, information quality, and whether the professional-facing handoff is useful.

**Before starting**

- Invite a volunteer, explain what will happen, how feedback will be recorded, and that they may stop at any time.
- Use the public fictional sample (HydroSeal) and demo route. No personal disclosures or unsent invention descriptions.
- Obtain permission before recording screen/audio; default to **no recordings**.
- Show the educational-only disclaimer and privacy pages. Keep any names or private notes out of GitHub and public issue comments.
- Record the session date, participant type, and a pseudonymous study code in a private approved location.

## Inventor session

| Time | Activity | Observation |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–2:00 | Explain objective, fictional-only data, no legal advice and ability to stop | Consent and expectations understood? |
| 2:00–5:00 | Find Learn and explain what the tool does in their own words | Is purpose clear? What was misleading? |
| 5:00–11:00 | Walk through demo disclaimer, 5-step intake and packet generation | Step blockers, confusing wording, time to finish |
| 11:00–13:00 | Find the generated packet and identify next professional question | Can they identify useful gaps and next action? |
| 13:00–15:00 | Four short questions below | Document opt-in feedback |

Inventor questions:
1. Before the walkthrough, how prepared did you feel for an IP professional conversation (1–5)?
2. After the walkthrough, how prepared do you feel (1–5)?
3. Which screen or question was least clear, and why?
4. What, if anything, would make you more willing to use this to prepare for a real professional conversation?

**Do not interpret a fictional demo's completion as a real inventor packet completion.**

## Professional session

| Time | Activity | Observation |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–2:00 | Explain fictional sample, no legal opinion/endorsement requested | Understands scope? |
| 2:00–6:00 | Review fictional professional handoff packet | Locate invention overview, timeline, gaps, questions |
| 6:00–10:00 | Identify missing or redundant fields | At least three actionable observations |
| 10:00–13:00 | Compare against typical first-contact intake questions | Expected efficiency / potential additional work |
| 13:00–15:00 | Gather usefulness and optional follow-up | Permission for notes or quotes separately |

Professional questions:
1. What are the first three things you'd need to ask after reading this packet?
2. What's missing or likely to be misunderstood?
3. Compared with a typical unstructured first inquiry, would this reduce, increase, or not change your preparation effort? Why?
4. If you routinely use a baseline first-contact process, approximately how many minutes does that baseline take, and how many minutes would reviewing this fictional packet take? **Optional estimates only; not observed time savings.**
5. Would you review a future fictional revision? Would you allow your feedback to be summarized anonymously?

## After the session

- Note concrete product blockers and exact screen/step **without copying private user content**.
- Separate observed behavior from opinions and assumptions.
- Don't call the session a professional intake review of a real inventor.
- Treat time-saved as **not verified** unless there is a measured, comparable baseline, reviewer permission, and documentation.
- Confirm permission before attributing quotes, organizational names, or logos.
- Allow participants to request deletion/withdrawal of any stored research notes.
